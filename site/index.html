THE OWNER'S SIDEClick. Edit. Save.

Their website.
Their words.

Owners sign in, click the text they want to change, and save. Photos, videos and repeatable content live right on the page, too. You decide what they can edit.

THE DEVELOPER'S SIDE

One attribute.
Editable content.

Add snip to your HTML. Flea moves the content into JSON and connects it to the page editor.

Your markup, classes and layout stay in your hands.

FROM FOLDER TO WEBSITE

A short trip to live.

Build locally. Keep your work in Git. Publish to your own server.

  1. 01

    Make a site

    A clean folder with HTML, CSS and JSON. Start with your own design.

    flea new my-site
cd my-site
  2. 02

    Make it yours

    Edit your files and see changes in the browser. No frontend build step.

    flea dev
  3. 03

    Put it online

    Publish committed files over SSH. One Flea server can host multiple sites.

    flea deploy
