Use case: logo-brand. Create one polished landscape identity specimen sheet for a NEW logo concept for ItsyWeb, a small developer tool that adds layouts and publishing to ordinary HTML. This is a logo design exploration, not a website mockup. The exact joined brand name is "ItsyWeb", capital I and W, no space. Render that text impeccably. Design an original ownable flat, vector-friendly identity with impeccable optical spacing. On a clean solid background show a very large primary horizontal symbol-and-wordmark lockup in the upper two thirds. In a quiet smaller bottom row show the SAME identity in solid black on white, reversed white on black, and a standalone icon at small size. These are size and ink tests of ONE design, never alternative concepts. Generous margins and presentation worthy of a specialist identity studio. No typography annotations, no headings, no descriptions, no invented taglines, no gradients, no gloss, no 3D, no device or stationery mockups, no stock symbols, no familiar company marks. Only the logo name may be legible text. Establish the actual logo, not a mood board. Direction: an independent software maker's friendly spider mascot. A memorable, very compact vermilion spider, eight stout legs clearly organised around a single rounded body, a tiny confident face with two simple cutout eyes. Strong graphic silhouette, purposeful rather than cute baby cartoon; the outer legs suggest a little weaving motion. Beside it bespoke hand-drawn ItsyWeb lettering, chunky and slightly irregular with subtly flared terminals, optical rhythm, warm charm and grown-up confidence. Vermilion #df3d26 logo on warm white #fff7e8. The spider must be simple enough to recognise at 24 px; avoid fine web lines, literal spider webs, circles of nodes, code brackets and the incumbent woven-loop motif. Personality comes from the silhouette and letterforms, not decorative scribbles. The little character and confident handmade lettering must feel drawn by one designer.
