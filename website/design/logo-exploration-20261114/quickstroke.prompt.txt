Use case: logo-brand. Create one polished landscape identity specimen sheet for a NEW logo concept for ItsyWeb, a small developer tool that adds layouts and publishing to ordinary HTML. This is a logo design exploration, not a website mockup. The exact joined brand name is "ItsyWeb", capital I and W, no space. Render that text impeccably. Design an original ownable flat, vector-friendly identity with impeccable optical spacing. On a clean solid background show a very large primary horizontal symbol-and-wordmark lockup in the upper two thirds. In a quiet smaller bottom row show the SAME identity in solid black on white, reversed white on black, and a standalone icon at small size. These are size and ink tests of ONE design, never alternative concepts. Generous margins and presentation worthy of a specialist identity studio. No typography annotations, no headings, no descriptions, no invented taglines, no gradients, no gloss, no 3D, no device or stationery mockups, no stock symbols, no familiar company marks. Only the logo name may be legible text. Establish the actual logo, not a mood board. Direction: independent cycling graphics and fast sign-painting adapted into a web tool identity. Original compact abstract W mark from three broad flat diagonal strokes, a decisive forward cut and one open negative-space channel; it should feel like a single quick confident gesture, not a ribbon loop, lightning bolt, swoosh or stock fast-forward button. Exact wordmark ItsyWeb as bespoke wide, strongly slanted sans lettering, crisp angular terminals, aerodynamic curves, excellent readability. A sharp expressive silhouette and an unusual W connect mark and lettering. Vivid orange #ff612e logo on deep plum #30152c. Generous quiet space around a forceful identity. Keep it two dimensional and solid ink. Avoid gradients, bevels, motion blur, extra speed lines, rockets and corporate-tech gloss. The smaller tests show the exact same W and lettering, not alternate symbols.
