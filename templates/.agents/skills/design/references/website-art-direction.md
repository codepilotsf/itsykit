# Website art direction

Read for new marketing sites, portfolios and substantial visual redesigns.
Use this inside Impeccable's direction, comp and build workflow, not as a second
tournament or approval round. Routine edits and task-focused app UI do not need it.
The brief and approved identity win; greater complexity is useful only when it
makes the subject more compelling, understandable or memorable.

## Establish what makes this site its own

For an existing-site rebuild or redesign, first settle the content path through
[website messaging](../../copywriting/references/website-messaging.md). In the
messaging path, establish the visitor's need, central promise, supporting evidence,
CTA and content sequence before visual exploration. Those decisions guide the
composition, imagery and hierarchy; do not fill an already-chosen layout with
rewritten copy afterward. The exact-copy path uses the current words as its
design material. Use the selected content baseline in comps and build reviews.

Identify the audience, offer or work, primary visitor action, available content
and approved brand constraints. Separate those from starter-template styling.
Name what the visitor should remember after leaving and which content can carry
that impression. Avoid choosing a mood adjective and decorating a standard page.

Build the page's argument from its content. A project archive, product
demonstration, service explanation and destination story need different structures.
Keep sections that earn their place; do not start every site from hero, logos,
three feature cards, testimonials, FAQ and closing CTA. Preserve required content
and proven existing patterns when scope calls for it. Neither minimalism nor
maximalism is a universal mark of quality.

## Use references to discover different answers

Use supplied references and saved collections first, within their stated scope.
For Mobbin, follow its installed search guidance before calls. Start with the
actual section and job in natural language, without prescribing the solution.
Do not add expected cards, accordions or a named brand unless the brief calls for
them. Marketing sections belong in `search_sections`, not app-screen searches.

For substantial exploration, seek a small useful spread of inspected references
across different sites, rather than many captures of one product. A useful
starting range is four to six references, not a quota. Let the evidence cover
different open questions: opening composition, typography and imagery, then
lower-page rhythm or a key interaction. Search the relevant section separately
when needed. An adjacent field can supply composition when the current category
is repetitive; identify it as structural inspiration, not domain evidence.

Change a weak query or use the tool's pagination/exclusions for another pull.
Do not spend the entire exploration budget on two near-identical hero searches.
Make a targeted follow-up only for an unresolved decision. Stop when directions
have enough evidence, or disclose the gap if focused follow-ups remain unhelpful;
do not browse indefinitely to fill a count. Honor any user budget or deadline.

For each retained reference, note its direct `mobbin_url` or original page URL,
what was actually inspected, and the particular decision it informs. For example:
how an image crosses a type column, how a lower section changes density, or how
navigation remains usable around an expressive opening. Name what stays behind:
its branding, claims, literal assets or irrelevant interaction. A screenshot
shows a static state; it cannot establish animation or responsive behavior.
Inspect the live original when those properties matter and access permits.
Choose a coherent direction from this evidence rather than assembling one copied
section from each reference. Supporting examples can resolve details without
introducing a competing visual system.

Mobbin provides shipped UI and section evidence; it need not supply every part
of a website's art direction. When a supplied gallery example or live site is
needed for typography, graphics or motion, inspect that source directly and label
it separately. Do not imply it came from Mobbin or work around a Mobbin refusal
to claim equivalent Mobbin results. No outside account mutations are needed.

## Make directions differ in substance

Work within Impeccable's dealt directions and the user's pinned constraints.
Before presenting, compare what the alternatives actually change: composition,
type voice and scale, graphic medium, density, color relationships, and motion
behavior. Alternatives with the same skeleton and a different accent are one
direction. Strengthen their structural differences without replacing assigned
worlds or re-rolling on taste. A fixed brand may keep type and palette constant
while composition and narrative do the exploring.

Give each direction a concrete first viewport and enough subsequent content to
prove it can sustain a page. Show real or honestly labeled illustrative copy.
Identify the dominant visual move, the content it serves and how it behaves on
mobile. Examples of possible moves include an art-directed photographic crop,
large expressive lettering, an animated diagram or a spatial project index.
These are possibilities, not a new menu every site must copy. Avoid making the
same typeface, green-and-cream palette or editorial layout the safe option in
every round. A repeated choice needs grounding in this brief, not habit.

Treat typography as part of composition: compare actual words in candidate faces,
including width, weight, optical size, line breaks and the relationship to body
copy. Check that the intended face and weights can actually be delivered before
promising them. Do not use an image model's invented lettering as proof that a
real font matches. Expressive scale may dominate a marketing opening when the
chosen world supports it; check loaded toolkit constraints before promising it,
and surface a material conflict rather than silently substituting safe UI type.

## Resolve the palette before asking for approval

Choose a coherent color relationship from the subject, imagery, brand or an
inspected reference. Name its intended temperature and contrast, then assign
roles: ground, main ink, supporting surface, action/accent and the semantic colors
the page needs. A muted palette, saturated field or multiple hues can all work;
no single hue, hue count or percentage formula is the default.
When an inspected reference supplies a convincing color relationship, preserve
the logic of its grounds, ink and accents as a starting point, adapted to the
approved world. Do not combine attractive swatches from unrelated references
and assume the resulting palette inherits their quality.

Judge colors on realistic content in the existing direction previews, not from
isolated swatches. Look at a large background, headline, body copy, action and
image together, plus the transition into the next section. Check:

- Lightness establishes hierarchy; neighboring tones do not merge into mud.
- Chroma and temperature support a clear dominant relationship rather than
  making every region equally loud or mixing unrelated undertones accidentally.
- The action remains visible, and imagery belongs beside the chosen surfaces.
- Foreground/background pairs pass the toolkit's contrast requirements at actual
  text sizes and states. Transparent overlays and text on imagery need checking
  over the composite background, including a difficult crop.
- The palette remains convincing across quiet content, a dense section and mobile.
  Dark/light variants, if required, each need deliberate roles.

Use Impeccable's colorize reference for role derivation, ramps and computed
contrast when needed; this does not add a separate colorize/polish workflow.
Contrast is a measurable floor; harmony still needs visual judgment. Do not
claim that complementary hue math or OKLCH values certify a beautiful palette.

Record the main color roles in the direction's existing contract and carry them
into comps and asset prompts. Keep prompts concise, using the approved visual
reference and a few essential color anchors rather than a conflicting list of
style adjectives. Inspect each generated preview before calling it ready for
selection: an image model's plausible recoloring is not an approved palette.
Serve required boards before generation, as the toolkit directs, then correct a
material mismatch in that same round. If the intended palette itself fails,
revise the direction before approval rather than asking the user to reject an
unresolved combination.
An approved palette stays fixed across compositional variants.

## Produce the visual idea instead of replacing it with boxes

Plan the assets and techniques that make the chosen direction work early.
Distinguish live text and controls, photographic or illustrated assets, geometric
graphics and motion. Keep text and interaction semantic. Use appropriate raster
generation or sourced imagery for picture-like work; CSS, SVG, canvas or WebGL
can produce geometry, masks, diagrams and interactive graphics when appropriate
to the toolkit and stack. A complex technique earns its cost through the visible
result. Do not replace the promised image with a gradient blob, or a graphic
background with a stock card merely because it is easier to implement.

When an image concept is unresolved, use the installed art-direct skill; use
photoreal-image for photographic execution. Apply the selected page palette to
new illustrative assets, and preserve factual constraints on documentary imagery.
Build-path choice does not remove the need for authored assets. Keep exact prompts
and source provenance project-local through the toolkit's asset workflow.

Design motion as part of the experience when it contributes: what triggers it,
what moves or changes, what remains readable, and the reduced-motion/static
equivalent. Do not add identical scroll reveals to every section. A still comp
does not deliver an animation promise; implement and exercise the behavior.
Use progressive enhancement, preserve navigation and scrolling, and budget image
size, font loading and animation work for the devices the site serves.

In the existing batched render review, compare the opening and representative
lower content against the selected direction, then check narrow screens and real
interactions. Judge the actual type, palette, assets, section rhythm and motion,
as well as contrast and overflow. Diagnose a missing visual idea at that level;
more padding and rounded corners cannot repair a lost composition. Keep the
upstream review limits and report any remaining material loss honestly.
